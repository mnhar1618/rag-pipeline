Company Refund and Support Policy

Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of their original purchase. To request a refund, the customer must provide their order number and the email address used during the purchase.

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the request has been approved. The refund is returned to the original payment method.

Refunds are not available for products that were purchased more than 30 days ago. Digital products that have been fully downloaded may also be excluded from the refund policy.

Technical Support

Customers can contact technical support from Monday to Friday, between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Support is available through email and the company's online support portal.

The average response time for technical support requests is 24 hours during business days.

Account Policy

Customers can change their account email address from the account settings page. Changing the email address requires confirmation through the new email address.

Customers who forget their password can use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page. A password reset link will be sent to their registered email address.

Privacy

The company stores customer account information, order information, and support requests. Customer information is used to provide services, process orders, and provide technical support.
